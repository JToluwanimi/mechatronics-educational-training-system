# Mechatronics Educational Training System

## 1. What is this?
A custom-built, modular training workbench designed for studying electronic and electric drive systems. The system integrates power supplies, motor controllers, signal generators, and multiple circuit modules (Magnetic Switch, Multivibrator, Resistors, Transistors, Analog Power) into a single mobile platform.

## 2. Why was it built?
To provide a hands-on educational platform that bridges the gap between theoretical circuit design and physical implementation. It allows students to safely experiment with AC motor control, logic gates, and analog circuits.

## 3. System Architecture
- **Mechanical:** Heavy-duty steel frame with lockable caster wheels for mobility.
- **Electrical:** Integrated AC/DC power distribution. 
- **Control Modules:** AC Motor Controller, Magnetic Switch, Multivibrator, Resistor Banks (1.0 & 2.0), Transistor configurations, and Analog Power modules.
- **Instrumentation:** Integrated meters (Voltmeter, Ammeter, DC Current Meter).

## 4. Implementation & Assembly
- Fabricated the physical frame and mounted the rack system.
- Wired the terminal blocks, switches, and meters.
- Integrated the AC motor and drive control panel.
- *Status: Fully Assembled / Tested.*

## 5. Testing & Results
- Verified continuity and voltage levels across all modules.
- Tested the AC motor controller with the mounted induction motor.
- **Limitations:** The system is a static trainer. It does not currently feature digital data acquisition (DAQ) for automated data logging.

## 6. Lessons Learned
- **Thermal Management:** Ensuring proper ventilation for the power supply modules during extended operation.
- **Safety Protocols:** Implementing emergency stop buttons and proper grounding for high-voltage AC sections.
